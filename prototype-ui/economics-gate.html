Types of Plans

RAP protection programs expand your sales team’s protection toolbox by letting them match the correct type of protection plan to best fit a customer’s needs. For millennial shoppers, budget-minded customers or single-item purchases, offer a $19.99 subscription plan. For purchases over $5,000 or where customers may want a longer program, offer your existing multi-year plan (or a RAP multi-year plan). RAP’s subscription and multi-year plans qualify for reinsurance programs.

Subscription

The customer pays $19.99 a month for the same coverage as a multi-year plan, and can cancel, start or restart at any time. The dealer earns a recurring commission on every successful monthly payment. Custom coverage available — talk to us to learn more.

Multi-Year

The customer pays an average of $250 once, and the protection income is earned at the original sale. If they cancel, a prorated share is returned. Custom coverage available — talk to us to learn more.
