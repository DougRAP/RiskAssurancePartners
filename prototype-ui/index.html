Multi-Year Protection

Protect the purchase they’ve been waiting to make.

When customers find the sectional they’ve been looking for or invest in a new bedroom set, many want to take care of protection at the same time. With one upfront payment, they get two to five years of comprehensive coverage. It’s a natural fit for larger purchases and for shoppers who prefer to pay once and know exactly how long their coverage lasts.

On average, 25% to 30% of customers choose a multi-year plan. RAP customizes coverage to fit your assortment and the way you sell, with programs for furniture, rugs, adjustable beds, mattresses, and outdoor furniture.

Monthly Subscription Protection NEW

Full protection for a home that gets lived in.

Juice cups, kids climbing on the cushions, and a dog at their feet. For young families, that’s everyday life. With full coverage starting at $19.99 a month, shoppers can protect the furniture they use every day while keeping their upfront costs down. They can cancel or restart their subscription as their needs and budgets change.

Roughly 70% to 75% of customers pass on a multi-year plan. A monthly option gives your sales team a second way to offer protection that fits how many millennials, young families, and budget-conscious shoppers prefer to pay. Subscriptions are available for furniture, rugs, adjustable beds, mattresses, and outdoor furniture.

Reinsurance

Reinsurance That Builds Wealth for Retailers

Every protection plan you sell can help build long-term wealth for your dealership. With RAP’s reinsurance program, you own the reinsurance company behind your protection plans. After covered claims and program expenses are paid, your company keeps the underwriting profit an insurance company would typically retain. You can invest those profits so they have the opportunity to grow as you continue selling plans.

Every RAP program is backed by an A-rated insurance carrier. RAP and its blue-chip partners manage the program from setup through claims, reporting, and trust administration. Your team focuses on selling protection plans, and your dealership shares in the financial upside of the business it generates.

Why switch to RAP

Five Challenges Facing Furniture Retailers and How RAP Helps

The challengeHow RAP helps
  1. 1

    Weak demand for furniture.

    Fewer home sales mean fewer households buying a roomful of furniture. Dealers have to cover their costs with fewer of those high-value orders.

    →

    How RAP helps

    Sell protection to the 70% that decline coverage.

    RAP gives you more program options to appeal to more customers: a multi-year plan for customers wanting one price and monthly subscriptions for budget minded and younger shoppers.

  2. 2

    Limited room to raise prices as margins tighten.

    Average retails have been pushed to their limits, adding another price increase can cost sales. Promotions, financing fees, and delivery costs reduce profit even when the ticket is bigger.

    →

    How RAP helps

    Keep more profit on each order.

    RAP’s cost-plus pricing lets you pay less for comparable coverage. Use a RAP reinsurance program and retain and invest underwriting profit in your own reinsurance account to extend your profits and build wealth.

  3. 3

    Commercial credit is expensive.

    Dealers need capital to buy inventory, cover payroll, and bridge slow periods. Tighter approvals, more collateral, and higher borrowing costs can squeeze cash.

    →

    How RAP helps

    Use reinsurance as a source of working capital.

    Eligible reinsurance programs can take loans against earned premium and repay interest to their accounts. When credit is stretched, RAP reinsurance programs can provide an alternate source of funding.

  4. 4

    Tariffs make costs harder to predict.

    A sofa advertised at one price today can increase in cost with the next shipment. The only choice is to absorb the cost increase without raising the price, which reduces retail margins.

    →

    How RAP helps

    RAP protection: no inventory and stable prices.

    Generate more profits with RAP protection programs: No inventory, cost plus pricing, no remit subscription plans, dealer recapture of underwriting profits, and dealer loans against earned premiums.

  5. 5

    Overhead costs keep running when sales slow.

    Rent, salaries, insurance, and other overhead come due every month. When sales are soft, every sale has to carry more of that cost while competitors get more aggressive.

    →

    How RAP helps

    RAP’s cost plus protection programs help close the gap.

    Four ways to generate profit and close the gap: Cost plus pricing means more coverage for less; no-remit subscription recurring revenue, sell and earn commissions; reinsurance profits returned to retailers; and best in class training and reporting tools increase close rates.
