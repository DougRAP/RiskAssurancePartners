How plan types and underwriting types combine

Protection plan types — how the customer buys

Subscription — $19.99 a month, cancel at any time. Multi-Year — on average $300 one time, with a prorated share returned on cancellation.

Underwriting types — who keeps the underwriting profits

Reinsurance — the dealer shares in the underwriting profits and gets tax benefits. Standard — RAP keeps them.

These are two dimensions, not four products — a plan type and an underwriting type combine. FurnitureRx is a product of Risk Assurance Partners.

What changed

The customer may not be saying “no to protection.” They may be saying “not another large purchase today.”

Two valid outcomes — not one right and one wrong

Upfront format fits

Multi-Year Protection

One complete decision at the point of sale. This is the sale that works, and it stays the first ask.

Upfront format doesn’t fit

FurnitureRx

Roughly 70% of customers say no to Multi-Year plans. This is that group — customers who want protection, but not another large purchase today. Source: RAP program experience
