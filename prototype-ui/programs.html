Subscription · protection plan type

FurnitureRx Subscription

Another way for the customer to say yes.

Sample copy — owner supplies final. A monthly protection plan for the customer who wants protection but not another large purchase on the day they buy furniture. The same coverage as a multi-year program, paid monthly.

The customer can cancel, start, or restart at any time, so they stay in control of the relationship. Roughly 70% of customers say no to Multi-Year plans; this is a way to earn income from that group. Source: RAP program experience

One decision, made once.

The customer pays on average $300 one time. If they cancel, they receive a prorated share back.

Reinsurance · underwriting type

Reinsurance

Keep the underwriting profit.

Sample copy — owner supplies final. An underwriting structure that pairs with a protection plan. Rather than the underwriting profit staying with the vendor, the dealer takes a share of it, along with tax benefits.

The value accrues over time rather than arriving as commission income does, so this is a long-horizon position rather than immediate profit.

Tax treatment depends on your circumstances — consult your own tax advisor.
