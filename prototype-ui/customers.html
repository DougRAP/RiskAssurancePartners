Good to know

When something happens.

Report it soon

Don’t wait — file as soon as you notice a stain or damage.

Blot, don’t rub

For spills, blot gently with a clean white cloth. Rubbing can spread the stain.

File before you fix

Contact us before trying a repair or a cleaning product, so we can point you to the right next step.
