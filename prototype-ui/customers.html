Customer Service

Need service on your protection plan?

Risk Assurance Partners administers the protection plan you bought with your furniture. File a claim online, or call and talk to our team.

File a Claim → Call 1.800.732.5856

Customer service hours: 9 AM – 6 PM ET, Monday–Saturday.

How a claim works

Three steps to get service.

Step 1

Gather your details

Have your sales receipt or plan paperwork handy, and take a few clear photos of the damage.

Step 2

File your claim

File online in a few minutes, or call us and we’ll take the details by phone.

File a claim online →

Step 3

We take it from there

Our claims team reviews your claim and contacts you with the next step.

Good to know

Before you file.

When something happens

  • Report it soonDon’t wait — file as soon as you notice a stain or damage.
  • Blot, don’t rubFor spills, blot gently with a clean white cloth. Rubbing can spread the stain.
  • File before you fixContact us before trying a repair or a cleaning product, so we can point you to the right next step.

About your coverage

  • Coverage depends on your planYour plan terms list exactly what is and isn’t covered.
  • Can’t find your paperwork?Call us and our team will help.
  • Bought your plan at a furniture store?You can come straight to us for service — we administer the plan for your retailer.
