Introducing Subscription Protection Plans

Generate Recurring Revenue From Your Protection Program

What is it?

A monthly protection plan subscription program that complements your current multi-year protection programs. Similar coverage to your multi-year program, but for a low monthly cost of $19.99/month.

How is it merchandised?

Subscription plans complement your multi-year programs by expanding options for your customers. On average 70% say “no” to multi-year protection. Subscription captures a slice of that 70% — converting lost sales into recurring revenue for you.

Who is it for?

Millennial shoppers, budget-minded consumers, lower-priced items, and anyone experiencing sticker shock or who wants more control over their protection program.

How is it sold?

  1. Your associate offers the multi-year plan. The customer says “no” or balks at the price. Your associate offers a low, cancel-anytime $19.99/month plan instead.
  2. Millennials are 55% of customers — budget-conscious, with families. Your associate reads the customer and offers subscription first, knowing the lower monthly price lets the customer spend on merchandise instead of protection.
  3. Your customer is buying a recliner, a single item, or something lower-priced. Multi-year isn’t a good fit budget-wise. Your associate offers $19.99/month.

How does the sales process work?

Set up a subscription SKU in your POS. When the customer says yes, ring one plan at $0 retail. Scan your QR code to open the kiosk on any device, or open it on desktop. Select the plan, enter a few details — sales order number, optional associate number — and the customer pays through Stripe. Scanning the QR is a safe, convenient way for the customer to enter their own card. That’s it. Coverage is bound.

How much do you make, and what does it cost?

Each time a subscriber’s auto-pay runs you earn $8, deposited by Stripe directly into your Stripe account. You owe RAP $0. There is no cost. Sell 200 new subscribers each month for 36 months and you’ll earn more than $1M in found money.

Does it qualify for your reinsurance program?

Yes — route all of your subscription revenue into your reinsurance program. A multi-year customer produces on average $30 in reinsurance reserves. A subscription customer produces $288 over 36 months — 9.6 times more. Best of all, funds earn in 30 days from each payment.

What about coverage?

Coverage closely mirrors your multi-year program. One subscription covers every type of item you sell, any number of pieces, up to a combined $5,000 retail limit.

What do your customers get?

Full coverage and the same RAP 4.5-star service they receive today. Customers can cancel, pause and restart coverage anytime. Customers are in control.

How do you start?

We already set up an account for you. Schedule a Zoom training and start selling immediately.

Why now?

You are already competing with subscription programs. Amazon sells one on its own site for $16.99. Today’s higher prices and stressed consumers make lower monthly payment options more important than ever.
